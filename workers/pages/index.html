How It Works
1
Install
Run the MSI installer. OpenFrequency creates its config in AppData on first launch.
2
Configure
Add your Gemini API key (free tier) and select your simulator in the Settings page.
3
Fly
Load a SimBrief plan, spawn your aircraft, and press Push-to-Talk to speak to ATC.
4
Immerse
Real cabin crew, bilingual ATIS, radar vectoring, taxi guidance — full flight experience.
